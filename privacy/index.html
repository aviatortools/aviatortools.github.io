Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Introduction

Aviator Calculator ("the app") is provided by Aviator Tools. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

The app is designed to perform flight calculations locally on your device. We are committed to keeping your data private and to collecting as little information as possible.

Data Collection

Aviator Calculator does not require you to create an account to use its calculators, and it does not collect personal data from the calculations you perform.

The inputs and results you enter are stored only on your device using local storage so your values can be preserved between sessions.

Purchases & Subscriptions

Aviator Calculator offers optional in-app purchases.

Payments and billing are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. Aviator Tools does not collect, receive, or store your credit card, debit card, or other payment information.

RevenueCat is used to manage purchase status, subscription status, entitlement verification, and purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process limited transaction and device-related information necessary to provide these services.

Subscription management and cancellation are handled through your Apple account and App Store subscription settings.

Your Flight Calculations

Your flight calculation inputs and results are not shared with third parties for the purpose of performing the calculations. Calculations are performed locally on your device.
